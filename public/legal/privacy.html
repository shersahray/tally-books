2. Two ways Sumlora runs, and what that means for your data

  • On your own computer or your own server. Your books stay on your computer or your server. We do not receive them, see them or store them. This policy then covers only the little we receive from you directly, such as your name and email when you buy a licence or write to us.

4. Where your information is stored

When we host Sumlora for you, your books, attachments and backups are stored in Canada, in Microsoft Azure’s Canada East region (Quebec City). A copy of each day’s backup is kept in separate Azure storage in Canada, so one problem cannot destroy both.

Some features send information outside Quebec or outside Canada, as the next section explains. Before any such transfer, we assess whether the information will be adequately protected, as Quebec law requires.

6. How we keep it safe

  • Connections to Sumlora are encrypted (HTTPS), and Azure encrypts the stored data.
  • Passwords are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. Five wrong passwords lock an account for 15 minutes.
  • Two-step sign-in is available for every user, and we require it on our hosted service.
  • Each user sees only the companies and features their role allows. Clients see only their own company.
  • Every change to the books is recorded in an activity log.
  • Backups are made every day and kept in a separate storage location in Canada.
  • Only [Your full name] and staff who need it to run the service can reach the servers, and they are bound by confidentiality.

7. How long we keep it

  • While your subscription is active: we keep your books and account for as long as you use Sumlora.
  • After you cancel: you have [60] days to download your books (the year-end package or a backup). We then delete them from our servers.
  • Backups: deleted books disappear from backups within [30] days, as old backups expire.
  • Billing records: kept for 6 years, because tax law requires it.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you, and give you a copy, including in a commonly used format you can take elsewhere;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • stop using or delete information we no longer need, or withdraw your consent, subject to legal and contract limits;
  • explain how we handle your information.

Write to our privacy officer at shersahray@outlook.com. We answer within 30 days. If your information is in books a firm keeps for you, we will pass your request to that firm, because they decide what happens to it.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9. If something goes wrong

If a confidentiality incident (a data breach) happens, we record it in our incident register and act quickly to limit the harm. If it creates a risk of serious injury, we tell the affected people and the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec, and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada where federal law applies. For data a firm keeps in Sumlora, we tell that firm right away so it can meet its own duties.

10. Changes to this policy
